Welcome to 86cn.

In this fast-paced world, we try to press pause here — to record life with care, and to present this land with tenderness.

Here you'll find both the grandeur of distant places and the small moments right beside us.

We'll set out with a backpack chasing the wind along the journey, and crouch down to photograph a flower blooming. We read good books by lamplight, and write a few insignificant midnight musings.

I am drawn to the quietness of minimalism, yet I also crave the freshness of constant reinvention. Updates may be slow, but every step counts.

May this little corner grow like a warm amber light — slowly, steadily — until it holds all the "Beautiful China" you see in the world.
